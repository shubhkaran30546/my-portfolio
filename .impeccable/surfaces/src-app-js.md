# Portfolio home (single page)

Mode: Experience (portfolio). Audience: hiring managers and senior engineers skimming for about 20 seconds. Action: email (primary), then résumé, GitHub, LinkedIn.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Dusk Almanac. The portfolio is an ephemeris for a running system: precise tables about warm light. It refuses the centered-hero-plus-card-grid dev template and the cold-blue terminal.

OWN-WORLD: Dusk indigo-charcoal ground with sodium-amber accent, haze-pink and sage as quiet secondaries. Archivo uses its width axis for tension (expanded display, normal body). Fragment Mono is reserved for times, versions, and measurements. Structure comes from hairline rules and almanac tables, with no cards and no glass.

STORY: The visitor learns that he keeps real systems alive at 150K+ users and 99.9% uptime, believes it from the changelog proof, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name is enormous on two staggered lines, top-left. The sunset plate is cropped tall on the right and overlaps the name. Above it sits a status strip: Operational, Edmonton, ticking time, computed sunset, Currently. Below it are the positioning line, the email CTA, and the résumé link.

FORM: Almanac × status page. Candidate 3 of 7. Seed f888c930. Raises: an unbroken amber scroll line (step row), a specimen size ladder (Emigre), one sign line per section (wayfinding), one huge word against tiny margin labels (shader portal).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
